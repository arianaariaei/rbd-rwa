# Pre-submission audit (2026-09-26)

A reviewer's read of the whole project: code, data, analyses and drafts. Each item says
what the problem is, why a reviewer would care, and its status (FIXED / DONE = addressed
in this round; TEXT = needs wording in the manuscript; OPEN = recommended work).

## A. Problems in the evidence

1. **Age confound in the RBD comparison (found in this audit).** CAP publishes ages in
   `gender-age.xlsx`, which the pipeline had not used. RBD patients are 71 +/- 6 years; the
   "same-system patients" are mostly epilepsy (NFLE) patients aged 30 +/- 11, and CAP's
   healthy volunteers are 32 +/- 5. RWA rises with age, so AUCs, Youden cut-offs and CAP
   false-positive rates compare old RBD patients with young controls. FIXED: ages are now
   read (`src/fetch.py: cap_demographics`), reported in T1, and T17 / FC9 compare RBD with
   same-system patients aged >= 50 (insomnia, PLM, sleep-disordered breathing, older NFLE).

2. **Spectrum and incorporation bias in sensitivity.** CAP's RBD patients have established,
   clinically diagnosed RBD, and the diagnosis itself requires PSG evidence of RWA, so
   sensitivity is optimistic for the screening target (prodromal, milder RWA). This makes
   our low PPVs conservative, which should be said. TEXT (Methods 2.1, Limitations, STARD
   10b/21a).

3. **"Flagged" is not "false positive" in MGH/HMC.** Undiagnosed RBD and isolated RWA (older
   age, antidepressants) exist in clinic populations, and antidepressant use is higher in
   the US than in the Netherlands - which would, if anything, make MGH truly higher than
   HMC. The cross-site comparisons assume similar true prevalence; the within-night ladder
   does not, and should be presented as the primary causal evidence. TEXT.

4. **Gain.** The noise explanation needs no assumption about gain (relative criteria are
   gain-invariant), but any absolute or floor-corrected rule does. DONE: FC6 shows that,
   with the noise removed, MGH, HMC and CAP record the same muscle activity at the same size
   (N2/N3 median 1.60, 1.70, 1.61 uV; REM median 0.44, 0.45, 0.39 uV).

5. **The MGH ladder subset is not representative.** The first 300 records are quieter and
   more often flagged than the rest (level 0.56 vs 0.61 uV; SINBAR 83 vs 73%). DONE (FC8);
   TEXT: say so where ladder percentages are quoted; paired results are unaffected.

6. **Unresolved rule parameter.** Montreal phasic bursts up to 10 s or 15 s (sources
   differ). DONE (FC7): 0-9 nights change per cohort; immaterial.

7. **Unit-less recordings under absolute rules (bug, found in this audit).** Three CAP
   healthy recordings are stored as raw converter counts; the floor-corrected rule treated
   them as microvolts. FIXED: FC now requires physical units (as the RAI and the 10-uV tonic
   limit already did); re-run.

8. **No artefact rejection beyond heartbeat and event epochs.** Human scorers discard
   movement, snoring and electrode artefacts; automated scoring counts them. Event-free REM
   (T5, T13) covers apnoea- and arousal-related activity only. OPEN (optional): a simple
   amplitude/saturation screen as a sensitivity analysis; otherwise TEXT (limitation).

9. **REM scoring in RBD.** Scorers may not score the most active REM epochs as REM, which
   dilutes RWA in RBD; CAP uses R&K rules, MGH/HMC AASM. TEXT (limitation; T15 addresses the
   transitional epochs).

10. **Exploratory vs pre-specified.** Two analyses were pre-registered (noise ladder;
    floor-corrected rules). Everything else - background definitions, strata, REM
    boundaries, B sensitivity, age - is exploratory and must be labelled so. TEXT.

11. **Reporting.** STARD items still open: flow diagram, 2x2 tables, retrospective design,
    missing data counts, funding. TEXT/OPEN (see stard_checklist.md).

12. **PPV hid the site effect.** T6 pooled MGH and HMC specificity. FIXED: per hospital.

## B. What most raises the paper

1. **A tested remedy (DONE this round).** The pre-registered floor-corrected rule removed
   the noise dependence (0 of 280 MGH nights changed at HMC's floor, Montreal-FC) and the
   site gap (Montreal +20.5 -> +1.6; SINBAR +51.0 -> +2.4), kept SINBAR's sensitivity (95%),
   and a SINBAR-FC cut-off re-derived on CAP transferred between hospitals (13% vs 14%
   flagged, 86% leave-one-out sensitivity). Montreal-FC missed the sensitivity criterion
   (81% vs >= 85%). This turns the paper from "a problem" into "a problem, its mechanism,
   and a validated fix".

2. **External validation on new open cohorts (OPEN, highest value next).** Test out of
   sample that (a) published-criteria positivity tracks each site's floor and (b)
   floor-corrected positivity does not. Request-free candidates checked:
   - BOAS (OpenNeuro ds005555, CC0): 128 nights, healthy drug-free adults, Micromed PSG,
     bipolar chin EMG, 3-scorer AASM consensus. Best candidate (healthy: no RBD expected).
   - Dreem DOD-H / DOD-O (Zenodo 15900394, MIT licence): 25 healthy, 55 OSA; 22 + 36 GB zips;
     chin EMG to be confirmed in the h5 files.
   - DREAMS subjects/patients (Zenodo), ISRUC-Sleep (MEGA): smaller, 200 Hz.
   Pre-register predictions before downloading any of them.

3. **Explain why visual cut-offs over-call when automated.** Our non-RBD values are 3-5x
   Frauscher's visual controls. Visual scorers read chin EMG at a fixed display gain, so
   twitches of 1-2 uV are invisible: visual scoring carries an implicit absolute floor that
   an automated relative rule lacks. The floor-corrected rule restores one. TEXT
   (Discussion; hypothesis, not tested).

4. **The detection limit in one line.** "Activity above k x background" requires muscle
   activity of sqrt(k^2 - 1) x background: 3.9x the floor for Montreal phasic, 1.7x for
   SINBAR - about 1 uV at MGH and 2.6 uV at HMC for SINBAR. TEXT (Results/Discussion; a
   small table of implied microvolt thresholds per site).

5. **A two-number calibration report.** Every RWA threshold should come with the recording's
   quiet-REM level (noise) and its net N2/N3 tone (gain). Both are computed by the pipeline;
   package them as a small function for other labs. OPEN (small).

6. **Mechanism figure with real traces.** The same MGH night as recorded and with +1.5 uV of
   noise, with the published and floor-corrected thresholds drawn: small twitches cross the
   published threshold only on the quiet recording. OPEN (medium).

7. **Framing and venue.** Multi-site prodromal cohorts now pool RWA across centres (NAPS: 9
   North American centres; DREAMER: 4 Italian centres using RAI < 0.85), and Japanese studies
   report SINBAR thresholds far below Western ones (Sakuma 2025; Mihashi 2026: 11.3% vs
   27.2%). Our result offers a testable, non-ethnic explanation and a fix. Target: SLEEP or
   Sleep Medicine (methods / diagnostic accuracy). TEXT.

8. **Availability.** Code and derived packs should get a DOI (Zenodo) at submission; the
   user decides when to publish the repository (not pushed).
