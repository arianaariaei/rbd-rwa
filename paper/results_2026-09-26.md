## Observations to follow up

(All four were followed up the same day: see `results_followup_2026-09-26.md`. In short:
1. storage format and amplitude do not explain MGH >> HMC, but adding noise to MGH until its
quiet-REM level matches HMC's reproduces HMC's positivity; 2. cross-validated cut-offs keep
86-90% sensitivity for the Montreal/SINBAR measures; 3. the background definition moves MGH
Montreal positivity 13-47%; 4. the apnoea gradient lives entirely in event epochs.)

1. Same method, two clinics: Montreal 29% vs 9%, SINBAR 76% vs 25%. Ages are similar
   (MGH 54.7, HMC 53.9). Candidate hardware cause: MGH chin EMG is stored at 1 uV
   resolution, close to quiet-REM amplitude (~0.6 uV), so the "background" estimate sits
   at the quantisation floor and 2x/4x-background thresholds sit in the noise. Test:
   compare background levels and positivity against ADC resolution; simulate
   quantisation on HMC/CAP signals.
2. Re-derived thresholds transfer far better than the visual ones (SINBAR any: MGH false
   positives 76% -> 10%) - needs cross-validated estimates and CIs.
3. Background definition (p5 REM) is a choice: sensitivity analysis still to run.
4. Sleep apnoea raises false positives (OR ~1.2 per 10 AHI); removing event epochs
   helps Montreal/SINBAR a little.
