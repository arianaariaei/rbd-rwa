# Figure legends (revision draft, 2026-10-27)

Figures are submitted as separate PDF/TIFF files. Pre-specified analyses are labeled as such;
everything else is exploratory.

**Figure 1. Share of nights flagged positive by the published cut-offs, automated scoring.**
Percentage of nights flagged (95% Wilson intervals) by the Montreal criteria (tonic activity in at least 30% of 20-s epochs or phasic activity in at least 15% of 2-s mini-epochs), the SINBAR chin criterion ("any" activity in at least 18.2% of 3-s mini-epochs) and the REM Atonia Index (below 0.8). CAP RBD, patients with RBD (n = 21); CAP patients, patients with other sleep disorders on the same recording system (n = 51); MGH (n = 925) and HMC (n = 147), clinical cohorts. For CAP RBD the share is the sensitivity; for the other groups, the false-positive rate if none has RBD.
*Alt text:* Dot plot of the share of nights flagged by three automated RWA criteria in four groups; the two clinical cohorts differ by about 20 (Montreal) and 50 (SINBAR) percentage points.

**Figure 2. Quiet-REM level by cohort, and recordings re-made in software (exploratory).**
(A) Each night's quiet-REM level (5th percentile of 1-s RMS chin EMG in REM; log scale; boxes, median and interquartile range; whiskers, 1.5 times the interquartile range); dashed line, the 1-µV step in which MGH stores chin EMG. (B) Share of nights flagged (95% Wilson intervals): HMC as recorded, quantized to 1-µV steps, quantized and resampled to 200 Hz, scaled by 0.39, and scaled and quantized; MGH as recorded and with white noise added (SD 1.5 µV; first 300 records).
*Alt text:* Box plots of the quiet-REM level by cohort, MGH at about 0.6 µV and HMC at about 1.5 µV, and a panel showing that re-formatting HMC leaves positivity unchanged while adding noise to MGH lowers it to HMC's level.

**Figure 3. The noise ladder (pre-specified).**
Share of nights flagged by each published cut-off as white noise is added to the chin EMG of the same nights (0.5-3.0 µV in-band RMS), against the group's median quiet-REM level (log scale). Filled circles, as recorded; open circles, noise added; stars, every night harmonized to 2.0 µV. CAP RBD (n = 21) and CAP patients (n = 51) on CAP's main system; MGH, 277 of the first 300 Challenge 2018 records (at least 10 min of REM as recorded and at every step, and a non-zero quiet-REM level); HMC, all nights.
*Alt text:* Line plot in which the share of nights flagged falls as noise is added, for every group, with MGH's line passing through HMC's point.

**Figure 4. Muscle activity of known size added to nights from six recording systems (pre-specified).**
(A, B) For each night, the amplitude of added activity detected in half of the injected scoring units (A50), against the night's quiet-REM level (log-log): (A) Montreal tonic (20-s epochs; tonic episodes added), (B) SINBAR "any" (3-s mini-epochs; phasic bursts added). Open symbols, published rules; filled, floor-corrected (B = 1.0 µV; nights within its range). Black lines, white-noise predictions fixed before the run. (C) The REM chin EMG of the 21 CAP RBD patients added to the nights of each system, at full and half amplitude: share of these nights flagged by the published and floor-corrected Montreal criteria (95% Wilson intervals). (D) Share of nights flagged by SINBAR "any" (at least 18.2%) as the amplitude of added phasic bursts rises, by system; solid, published; dashed, floor-corrected.
*Alt text:* Scatter plots in which the amplitude needed for detection rises in proportion to the quiet-REM level under the published rules but stays near 2 µV under floor correction; dot plot of RBD activity detected on each system; dose-response curves by system.

**Figure 5. Recording systems held back for confirmation (pre-specified).**
Share of nights flagged by the published (open symbols) and floor-corrected (filled; arrows) SINBAR (A) and Montreal (B) criteria against each cohort's median quiet-REM level. Circles, patients (CAP same-system patients, MGH, HMC, DOD-O); squares, healthy volunteers (BOAS, first night per participant, n = 99; DOD-H, n = 25); star, CAP RBD.
*Alt text:* Two scatter plots in which published positivity falls with the cohort's quiet-REM level, reaching 91% in healthy volunteers on the quietest system, and floor-corrected positivity does not.

**Figure 6. Parkinson's disease research: continuous RWA and RBDtector (pre-specified).**
(A) Change in tonic chin RWA on the same 21 CAP RBD nights when the quiet-REM level is raised from 0.86 µV to 1.5 µV, mean with 95% bootstrap interval, under the published and floor-corrected Montreal criteria and RBDtector. Gray bars, published visually scored differences for scale: baseline tonic RWA in patients who later developed Parkinson's disease vs those who did not \[19\], and the rise over about five years in isolated RBD \[20\]. (B) RBDtector, run unchanged: share of nights flagged (chin "any" at least 23.5%) as noise is added, published baseline (dotted) and floor-corrected baseline (solid). (C) RBDtector as recorded, by system: published (open) and floor-corrected (filled).
*Alt text:* Bar chart showing a 21-point fall in tonic RWA under the published rule and about one point under floor correction, next to published Parkinson's disease differences of 32 and 24 points; line and scatter plots of RBDtector positivity by noise level and system.
